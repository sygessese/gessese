title: "On becoming"
date: "2026-07-05"
description: "I woke up late again"
---
I woke up late again, dipping in and out of sleep, in and out of thoughts, in and out of some vague sense that I’m missing something. 

I fill carts with beautiful things I don’t buy, or sometimes do. 

Everything I reach for is a placeholder, for a life that isn’t quite here yet, a self I haven’t fully become, a connection I haven’t met.
I’ve built a good life and I’m standing on the outside of it, not fully inside my body, inside my friendships, inside anything lately.

I want for something more, something I’ll remember when I see it, something that feels like, “Oh, there you are”.

It’s been missing for a while, and today I’m questioning everything, my habits, my purpose, the way I withhold experiences until I’ve earned it.
My conviction that only when I’m at the peak of all of my unattainable goals, then I’ll deserve the life I crave.

But what I’m sitting with today is that, even if I had the art studio, the curated closet, the soulmate, I’d still wake up with me. Me, with my longing, my grief, my hunger. And if the ache doesn’t go away when I get what I want, then maybe it’s not meant to go away, maybe it’s here to guide me toward something deeper, maybe my longing is a mirror to my personal fulfillment.

I believe part of that is expression. Not performance, not curation, but real, raw, imperfect expression. Like this, unfiltered, as it exists within me. I don’t know where I’m going, but I do know I’m tired of waiting to be “ready” before I do. 

This is me, in between, in the ache, in the becoming.
